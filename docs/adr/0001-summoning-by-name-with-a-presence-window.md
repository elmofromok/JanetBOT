# Summoning by name, with a presence window

Janet responds to her name appearing in a message, not only to an `@mention`,
and then stays Present in that channel so follow-up messages reach her without
another Summon. This is how the character works: anyone says "Janet" and she is
there until dismissed. Mention-only would have been the Discord-native and much
safer choice, since bare-word matching fires on ordinary conversation about her
and a presence window means Janet reads a channel while active. We took the
character over the convention deliberately. Anyone "fixing" this back to
mention-only is changing the product, not the implementation.
